---
title: "Identity starts in HR, not Active Directory"
date: 2026-10-18
tags: [iam, midpoint]
---

My lab began with Active Directory as the source of truth, which was a good way to learn import and correlation. Real identity programs flow the other way: HR creates the person, the IGA platform decides what they get, and AD is just a target.

So OrangeHRM is now the lab's authoritative source. Employee ID, not username, is the correlation key, because it never changes. Full write-up in [IAM Lab #7](/blog/iam-lab-07-orangehrm-hr-source).
